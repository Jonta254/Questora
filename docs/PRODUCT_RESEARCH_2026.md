# Product research — September 2026

Primary Pi Network material emphasizes real-world utility, functional completeness, app quality, policy compliance, and accessible participation. Pi's September 2026 developer update also documents device-local storage for eligible Pi Browser apps. Questora therefore keeps unpublished drafts device-local, avoids artificial rewards, and uses contribution history, meaningful activity, unanswered-question discovery, and draft continuation as its return loops.

Implemented from this review: a visible continue-draft card, transparent Recent/Unanswered/Most discussed feed sorting, and recent real contribution history on signed-in profiles. These features use existing user-owned data and do not infer KYC, trust, expertise, location, or popularity.
