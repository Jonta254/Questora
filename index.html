Support and policies

Clear expectations for a useful, safer community.

Community guidelines

Share relevant experience, explain uncertainty, and treat people with respect. Do not post harassment, scams, private information, or dangerous instructions.

Privacy

Public questions and answers are visible to everyone. Pi sign-in is requested only when you contribute. A Pi account does not prove KYC, expertise, or trustworthiness.

When you report something

Reports are private and include the selected reason, optional context, and the content being reviewed. Other community members cannot see report details. Do not include sensitive personal information in the optional note.

Payments and rewards

Questora does not currently request payments, offer rewards, or sell promotion. Treat any message claiming otherwise as suspicious and report it.

Support

This release is a pre-production community foundation. If something fails, your unpublished question draft remains on this device so you can safely retry.
